Dagster Pipes

Run an asset's compute in a foreign process, keep Dagster's logs, metadata and materializations.

1 · Two processes, two channels

context (Dagster → external, once) messages (external → Dagster, streamed) process launch

The external process needs only the tiny dagster_pipes package — no Dagster install, no network access to Dagster.

2 · Lifecycle of one step

3 · The step and the external process live and die together

This is the right trade for a Spark or Databricks job, which has no durability of its own. It is the wrong trade for a Temporal or Prefect workflow, where surviving the caller is the entire point.

4 · Logs stream · materializations do not

If the step dies before the external process finishes, the buffered materializations are lost even though the work was done.
